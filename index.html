About Us

Jan Seva Kendra has been delivering trusted digital services for more than 5 years. We are dedicated to simplifying essential government and semi-government services for every citizen.

Our center was established with a mission to remove the hurdles in accessing public services. From identity documents to utility bill payments, we ensure speed, accuracy, and transparency.

Under the leadership of Aman Gaud, the Founder and State Head of ZENPAY (Maharashtra), Jan Seva Kendra has grown into a trusted digital service provider known for professionalism and reliability.

ZENPAY, a major digital payment and e-governance platform, plays a big role in our operational excellence. As the State Head, Aman ensures our center stays updated with the latest tools, security, and high-standard workflow.

Our Co-Founders, Ravindra Das and Piyush Prajapati, bring strong technical and customer-focused experience. Together, they ensure efficient documentation, cyber services, and smooth support for thousands of users each year.

We now provide over 50+ government and digital services including: Aadhaar updates, PAN card services, MSME (Udyam) registration, Gumasta (Shop Act), passport assistance, ticket booking, income tax filing, utility payments, and more.

Our vision is simple — make government services easier, faster, and accessible to everyone.

We thank all our customers for trusting Jan Seva Kendra and promise to deliver excellent service every single day.
